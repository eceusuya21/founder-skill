### The founder's "about P3,000" claim

Partly true. At mall chains a plain gel manicure is 800 to 1,000, nowhere near 3,000. But a full extension set plus detailed art does land around 3,000:
- Nailaholics extensions 2,700 + hand-painted art 650 = about 3,350 ([source](https://salonpriceph.com/nailaholics-nail-salon-and-spa/)).
- Mich & Myl extensions 2,400 to 2,900 + 3D art set 900 = about 3,300 to 3,800 ([source](https://michmylnails.net/services/)).
- Posh builder gel with gel polish 3,320 ([source](https://booky.ph/biz/posh-nails-sm-megamall/menu/)).
- Okada Japanese foot care with art 3,000 to 3,200 ([source](https://dev-cdn-storage.okadamanila.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/04055050/The-Retreat-Spa-Nail-Art-Menu-0724.pdf)); Ash Luxe base 2,545 to 3,445 ([source](https://www.cosmo.ph/beauty/independent-nail-artist-philippines-a6296-20260107-dyn)).

No public source confirms a wedding nail price of 3,000; bridal nail pricing is simply not published. So: 3,000 is right for "extensions plus elaborate design", wrong for "nail design" in general.

## 4. Complaints, ranked

Public reviews for these businesses were scarce in search; Reddit was not accessible to the search tool and Google Maps reviews did not surface. Every theme below is **thin** (under three reviews each).

1. **Home service comes with minimums and extra fees** (thin, but stated by the providers themselves, 4 sources): "Transportation fees apply" ([Buffed](https://www.buffednaillounge.com/homeservice)); minimum consumable rates ([Celebrity Nails](https://celebritynailsph.com/)); "some areas may require min. worth of services" ([Nail Studio & Mobile Spa](https://www.beautynailhairsalons.com/PH/Antipolo/167823439930769/The-Nail-Studio-%26-Mobile-Spa)); separate 1,300 home service fee ([PrimeLash on Fresha](https://fresha.com/lp/en/tt/nail-polishing/in/ph-manila)).
2. **Mall gel feels pricey for what it is** (thin, 1): SPOT.ph's reviewer called Nailaholics' P1,000 "pricey" for a basic gel ([SPOT.ph 2025](https://www.spot.ph/shopping/top-list/top-10-gel-manicures-2025-a2793-20250808-dyn)).
3. **Rushed finishing / drying** (thin, 2): Nail Tropics reviewers say staff "didn't really help with the drying part" and polish came off because toenails "were still wet" ([Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/nail-tropics-makati)).
4. **Limited colour choice** (thin, 2): Nail Tropics "out of a lot of their nail polish colours" ([Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/nail-tropics-makati)); limited polish selection at The Secret Lounge ([ClassPass](https://classpass.com/search/philippines/nails)).
5. **Design-strong artists will not come to you** (thin, 2, from the artists' own pages): NailsByMARIA "NO HOMESERVICE" ([Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/nailsbymaria_15/)); All Hail Nails appointment only, no same-day ([TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@allhailnailsss_/video/7497642546965925128)).

## 5. The gap

Nobody in Cainta or nearby Rizal publicly sells **design-level nails (extensions, detailed art, bridal sets) delivered at home, with a published per-head entourage price.** The evidence:
- The home-service players (Go Spa PH, Celebrity Nails, Nail Studio & Mobile Spa) sell spa-style basics; the most design they list is "Nail Art (simple) P500".
- The design-led artists near Cainta (Mieux in Marikina, All Hail Nails and NailsByMARIA in Antipolo) work from home studios, and one says outright "NO HOMESERVICE".
- No bridal nail package or per-head entourage nail price was found anywhere in Metro Manila; brides currently piece it together from salon party packages (about 1,475 to 2,175 per guest) or HMUA teams (1,500 to 5,000 per face for makeup).
- Mall design work costs about 2,200 to 3,400 and requires the client to travel and sit for hours, which does not suit a wedding morning with 6 to 10 people.

The business can own: "bridal and event nail design at your home in Cainta and Rizal, with a clear per-head price and travel fee published up front." The published price list is itself a differentiator, since most local competitors hide prices or fees.

## 6. The threat

- **Fastest copy: independent design artists adding home service** (Mieux Nails in Marikina, the Antipolo home studios). They already have the skill and the Instagram portfolio; all they need is a kit and a travel fee. Defence: lock in the bridal workflow (trial session, timeline, multi-tech crew for entourages), which a solo artist cannot staff.
- **Home spa services upgrading design** (Go Spa PH, Celebrity Nails). They already cover Cainta and Taytay and have booking systems. If they recruit one design artist, they have reach you lack.
- **Bridal HMUA teams bundling nails.** They already sit in the wedding-day room and price per head. A partnership may be smarter than competing.
- **Press-ons** (55 to 5,000) undercut everyday nail art, which is why everyday art should stay an add-on, not the lead.

## Evidence strength

Gel and extension prices: fairly solid (8+ sources, mostly 2025 to 2026, though via search excerpts). Basic mani/pedi and home-service fees: thin. Bridal per-head nail pricing: none published. Reviews and complaints: thin across the board. Local Cainta Facebook techs: not discoverable by search; a manual Facebook and Google Maps scan is the next step.
