# Working name: Home Nail Studio (Cainta)

- What it is: a home-service nail business in Cainta, Rizal that brings mall-salon
  and wedding-quality nail design to the customer's home, plus basic nail care
  (cleaning, trim, cuticles) and full manicure/pedicure.
- Who it is for: women in Cainta and nearby Rizal / east Metro Manila who pay for
  nail art at mall salons, and brides and wedding entourages. Secondary: busy
  professionals and anyone who prefers not to travel to a salon.
- What it sells, at what price: the main selling point is nail design (the kind
  that costs about ₱3,000 at mall salons or for weddings, per the founder). Basic
  cleaning and mani/pedi as entry services. Price not set yet: to be found by
  /founder-competitors, /founder-consumer and /founder-pricing.
- Where and how: Cainta, Rizal, the technician travels to the customer's home.
  Booking channel not decided yet.
- Budget and constraints: ₱50,000 startup budget. The founder can give about 3
  hours a day and runs operations (booking, scheduling, routing, kits, quality);
  the nail design work is done by a skilled nail artist the founder will recruit
  (not yet named). Founder's strength is operations and workflows.
- Focus (from the board): lead with bridal and event nail design at home in Cainta
  and nearby Rizal; everyday nail art and basic care as add-ons and weekday fill.
