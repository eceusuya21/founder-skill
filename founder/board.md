# Board summary: Home Nail Studio (Cainta)

**Vote: 3 FUND IF, 0 pass. Average score 4.7 / 10.**
Offers 5, Monopoly 4, Product 5. Memos in `board/`.

## Risks more than one member raised
1. **Who does the nails is not decided.** All three. The technician's hands are
   the product. With no named tech and no portfolio, buyers have no reason to
   trust the work, and a hired tech can leave with the clients.
2. **No proof yet.** Offers and Product. Nail art is judged by eye. No portfolio,
   no reviews.
3. **Too broad a menu.** Offers and Product. Cleaning, trims, mani/pedi and design
   for everyone is a salon menu and a commodity that drifts into a price war.
4. **No route to customers.** Monopoly and Product. Booking channel and the path
   to the first 100 customers are undecided.
5. **Capacity.** Offers. Bridal work clusters on weekend mornings and one
   entourage can take hours; 3 hours a day may not cover peak demand.

## Conditions checklist
- [ ] Decide who does the nail work (founder or hired tech) and how the business,
      not the tech, owns the brand and client list.
- [ ] Portfolio of at least 10 real finished designs at mall-salon or bridal
      quality before launch.
- [ ] Lead with one thing: nail design at home, bridal and entourage first. Basic
      care only as an add-on.
- [ ] One named bridal package (e.g. bride + entourage on location, design preview
      by photo, touch-up kit).
- [ ] A written guarantee you can keep at peak (photo-approved design, on-time
      arrival, free fix within 48 hours).
- [ ] Write the full visit as a checklist (booking to follow-up) and run 5 test
      visits before charging full price.
- [ ] Name the booking channel and the path to the first 100 customers.
- [ ] One advantage a copycat can't match in 6 months (bridal referral system,
      signature style, or an ops standard).
- [ ] Check the ₱3,000 reference against real prices (/founder-competitors).
- [ ] Price per package from real data (/founder-consumer, /founder-pricing).
- [ ] Cost per visit incl. materials, travel and tech time (/founder-cfo).

## The board's disagreement
Monopoly scored lowest: it sees no moat in a business any skilled tech with a
phone can copy, and leans on referrals and ops know-how as the only durable edge.
Offers and Product are more hopeful because the bridal segment has real urgency
and money.

## The strongest version the board can see
Not the general home nail service as pitched. A **bridal and event nail design
studio that comes to you** in Cainta and nearby Rizal: bride plus entourage done
on location the morning of, design approved by photo in advance, on-time
guarantee, touch-up kit and free fix. Every bride brings an entourage and the next
bride. The founder's ops strength becomes the edge: a visit that runs like
clockwork, which is exactly where freelance techs are weakest. Everyday nail art
and basic care can come later, filling weekday slots.
