## Risks raised by more than one member

1. **The whole business is one technician on undefined terms** (all three). If they leave, get busy or take customers directly, nothing is left.
2. **Nothing sets it apart** (all three). With no name, no edge and no stated difference, it is a commodity that can only compete on price.
3. **Trying to do too much** (Offers, Product). Homes and businesses, cleaning and repair are several businesses at once.
4. **No plan for getting customers** (Offers, Monopoly). Where the first 100 customers come from, and what each costs, is unknown.

## Where they disagree

The Monopoly lens passes because nothing here can't be copied. The Offers and Product lenses say a commodity service can still win on a better offer and a better visit, if it is narrowed and written down. All three agree the technician deal comes first.

## Conditions (checklist for the rest of the pack)

- [ ] Written technician agreement: pay or profit share, quality and conduct standard, non-solicit of customers, what happens if they leave. (`/founder-ops`)
- [ ] Plan for a second technician. (`/founder-ops`)
- [ ] Pick one starting customer (households **or** small businesses) and one starting service (cleaning). (`/founder-consumer` tests which)
- [ ] A one-sentence named offer with a guarantee (e.g. free re-clean if the problem returns within a set period). (`/founder-offer`)
- [ ] Price on value, tested with real buyers, not just below competitors. (`/founder-competitors`, `/founder-pricing`)
- [ ] A one-page visit standard: booking, arrival, room protection, cleaning steps, before/after photos, clean-up, receipt, follow-up. (`/founder-ops`)
- [ ] One concrete edge buyers care about: maintenance plans, guaranteed response time, or a channel competitors don't use. (`/founder-offer`, `/founder-marketing`)
- [ ] A tested way to get customers with a known cost per customer. (`/founder-marketing`, `/founder-launch`)
- [ ] Proof from the first jobs (reviews, before/after photos) before spending on ads. (`/founder-launch`)
- [ ] Starting budget stated. (`/founder-cfo`)

## The strongest version the board can see

Not quite what was pitched. Start with **cleaning only, for households in a few Cainta subdivisions**. Sell a **maintenance plan** (e.g. scheduled cleans a year with priority repair booking) rather than one-off visits, so customers come back automatically. Make every visit the same high standard, with before/after photos, no mess and a reminder for the next one. Lock in the technician with a written profit-share deal, and train a second one early. Repairs and small-business accounts come later, once reviews and route density exist. The edge is not the cleaning itself. It's being the dependable, booked-ahead aircon service in one part of town.
