# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The bridal segment is the best market in the brief. Brides and entourages already pay for nail design, the event is high stakes, and the date creates real urgency. The founder's ₱3,000 reference is unverified.
- Home service cuts the time and effort parts of the value equation: no travel, no queue, and an entire entourage done in one place before the event.
- The founder's operations strength fits the hard part of a mobile service: scheduling, kits, travel routing and on-time arrival.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No proof, so likelihood is near zero.** Nail art is judged by eye. With no portfolio, no reviews and no named technician, a bride has no reason to trust a stranger with her wedding nails. It is not yet stated who does the work.
2. **A commodity offer that drifts into a price war.** "Basic cleaning and mani/pedi at home" is easy to copy and easy to undercut. If the only edge is convenience or a lower price than malls, margins vanish.
3. **Capacity versus a 3 hour day.** Wedding work clusters on weekends and early mornings, and one entourage can take hours. If the founder cannot deliver at peak, the guarantee breaks and so does word of mouth.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Who exactly does the nails, and can I see 10 finished designs?
- Which segment comes first: brides, or everyday nail art buyers? They need different offers.
- What is the guarantee? For example: designs approved by photo before the day, on time or a discount, and a free fix within 48 hours.
- Which bride problems (matching the motif, morning timing, chips before the reception) can you solve cheaply?
- Could you charge more than the mall salon and still be the obvious choice?
- Open numbers nobody has yet: actual mall and wedding nail prices nearby (/founder-competitors), how many brides and nail art buyers are in reach and what they will pay (/founder-consumer), cost per visit including supplies and travel (/founder-cfo), and the price of each package (/founder-pricing).

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. A named technician with a portfolio of at least 10 real designs before any launch.
2. Lead with one named bridal package (for example, bride plus entourage on location, design preview, touch-up kit), not a menu of basic services.
3. A written guarantee the founder can keep at peak weekend demand.
4. Prices set from /founder-competitors and /founder-cfo data, priced on outcome, never as the cheapest option.
