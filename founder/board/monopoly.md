# Monopoly lens
Score: 4 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- There is a small market that could be owned first: brides and entourages in Cainta and nearby Rizal who want mall-quality nail design at home.
- Wedding work comes in groups. One bride can bring a whole entourage, a natural low-cost distribution channel.
- The founder's strength is operations and workflows. A repeatable booking, travel and quality system is one of the few edges a service business can turn into know-how.
- A local brand built on wedding referrals is the most realistic durable advantage here.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No moat, easy copycats.** Home-service nails need no technology and little capital. Any skilled technician with a phone can offer the same thing in month six. Nothing here is ten times better yet.
2. **Distribution is undecided.** No booking channel, no plan for how the first 100 customers arrive or what each costs. A product with no way to reach buyers is not a business.
3. **The core skill is not owned.** It is not stated who does the nail work. If the design talent is hired, that technician can leave and take the customers.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Who else sells home-service or bridal nail design to the same Cainta buyer, and at what prices? (Open question: /founder-competitors.)
- Is the ₱3,000 reference real, and what will a Cainta customer pay at home? (Open question: /founder-competitors and /founder-pricing.)
- How do the first 100 customers arrive, and what does each cost to win? (Open question: /founder-consumer for the channel, /founder-cfo for acquisition cost.)
- How large is the yearly bridal and entourage market in Cainta and nearby Rizal? (Open question: /founder-consumer.)
- What is the secret: what do you know about this customer that salons and freelancers do not?
- Who does the nail work, and what keeps that person and the client list with the business?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Pick one small market to dominate first (for example, bridal and entourage bookings in Cainta) and show why this business can take a large share of it.
2. Name the distribution channel and a credible path to the first 100 customers, with a cost per customer from /founder-cfo.
3. Settle who does the nail work and how the business, not the technician, owns the brand and client list.
4. Name one advantage a copycat cannot match in six months: a bridal referral system, a signature design style, or an operations standard competitors lack.
