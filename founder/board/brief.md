## The idea, as the founder told it

The founder wants to start an aircon (air conditioner) cleaning and repair business.

- **Location:** Cainta, Rizal, Philippines (east of Metro Manila).
- **Customers:** residential households and small businesses, both from the start.
- **Services:** aircon cleaning and fixing, done at the customer's home or premises.
- **Price:** not decided. The founder said plainly they have no pricing in mind yet.
- **People:** the founder is not certified. Someone the founder knows who is a certified technician will be the main person doing the work at first. The founder's own role is not stated; presumably running the business side (bookings, marketing, customers).
