## What would flip a no

- A first-booking promo around ₱1,000 to ₱1,200, plus an Instagram or TikTok portfolio of real client nails that look as good as the designs I save.
- If I had a wedding or big event coming up, the venue service with the photo-approved design and the 48-hour chip fix would make me book. Or a simpler plain gel option closer to ₱700-800.
- An event coming up, plus proof of hygiene: a sealed sterilized tool pack opened in front of me and real reviews from women in Cainta I can check.
- A promo of about ₱800 to ₱1,000, or a group rate if my sister and cousins book with me for the wedding so it comes out cheaper per person.
- A first-booking price closer to ₱1,000 to ₱1,200, plus an Instagram or TikTok page with real client nails and a video showing how they sterilize their tools.
- A strong Instagram or TikTok portfolio with real client photos, plus an entourage group rate for my sister's wedding.
- A good number of real reviews and before-and-after photos from actual Cainta clients, especially ones that show how the nails looked two or three weeks later.
- If my suki couldn't do my wedding date and they had a group rate for the whole entourage, around ₱1,200 each, plus real customer photos and reviews from brides in Cainta.
- A real Instagram or TikTok portfolio with close-up videos of their nail art and tagged clients from around Cainta, plus an intro price for a first set.
- If my suki couldn't make the wedding date and they offered a group rate for the whole entourage with real client photos I could check, I'd consider it.
- Seeing how they actually sterilize their tools (sealed pouches opened in front of me, an autoclave, real reviews from brides) when we start planning nails for the whole entourage for my sister's wedding.
- A first-time or event promo around ₱1,000 with real customer photos showing the nails still intact after two or three weeks.
